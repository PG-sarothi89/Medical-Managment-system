Built around your complete workflow.

Every feature outlined in the APS Medical Management specification is live and functional.

Medicine Master

Generic names, compositions, HSN codes, unit of measure, and prescription status.

Batch & FEFO Engine

Expiry dates, batch pricing (Cost/Sell/MRP), and First-Expired First-Out automation.

Inventory Ledger

Audited chronological stock movement trail with reference IDs and balance reconciliation.

Point of Sale (POS)

Barcode scanning, multi-item cart, loyalty points, customer credit, and split payments.

Invoices & GST

GST-compliant invoice generation, thermal receipt print, and A4 print view.

Payments & Returns

Process sale returns, calculate refunds, and toggle automatic inventory restocking.

Supplier Management

Directory of pharmaceutical distributors, credit periods, and balance tracking.

Purchase Orders

Create purchase orders and convert to Goods Receipt with automatic batch creation.

Prescription Management

Register doctors, write digital prescriptions, and dispense directly into POS.

Customer Management

Track customer credit limits, outstanding balances, and reward loyalty points.

Reports & Forecasting

Profit margin analytics, fast/slow moving drugs, and demand forecasting models.

RBAC & Audit Trail

User administration, permission matrix, and continuous system audit logs.
